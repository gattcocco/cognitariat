Will the robots kill us all? Hard to say. As the Dude wisely opined in The Big Lebowski, “a lotta ins, a lotta outs, a lotta what-have-yous. And, uh, a lotta strands to keep in my head, man. 

But if keeping all those strands in your head is vexing, why not just make up a number and call it P(doom)? The term — shorthand for “probability of doom” — is suddenly mainstream. A couple of weeks ago Evan Hubinger, a senior AI safety researcher at Anthropic, tweeted, “we really do earnestly believe AI could kill all humans! I personally think it is >10% within the next decade”.     (Tim Harford, September 23, FT)
