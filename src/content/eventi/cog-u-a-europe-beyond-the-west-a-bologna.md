## **Saturday 14**

### **Morning**

 **9.30 – Assembly – Europe beyond the West within the Global Civil War**

In the global civil war regime, we recognize **Europe as the political and social space** in which to act and **confederate our struggles in order to reach a horizon of overall liberation**.

Europe is now caught between the **neoliberal project of the European Union** on the one hand, and the rise of **authoritarianisms and fascisms** on the other, with both actively driving processes of war and rearmament. We want to reverse this trajectory by building a **transfeminist, ecological, anti-capitalist and technological Europe for everyone**: a space in which to build **a movement against war without accepting any unjust peace**. We believe this is the minimum political and organizational horizon on which to position ourselves, without limiting ourselves to practices of resistance.

***

### **Afternoon**

### **14.30 – Assembly – Antifascism 2.0 is against remigration**

Authoritarianisms and fascisms are acting at a transnational level through increasingly structured forms of organisation. What primarily holds them together is the ideology of **remigration**: the fantasy of a purged or segregated society, in which those who are deemed to exceed its boundaries can be exploited, deported or suppressed. To be **effectively anti-fascist today**, we need to strike at this point by **organising at least at a European level** — since the national space is no longer sufficient to effectively confront remigration — and by denying the Black International any space to speak.

***

### **16.00 – Talks**

- **Reclaiming Tech & AI for people and the climate.**
The oligopolistic development of the tech sector is directing Artificial Intelligence and new technologies towards capitalist profit, turning data centres into energy-hungry monsters that deepen social and environmental inequalities. We need to reverse this direction: **AI and new technologies** must serve the **common good** and the **fight against climate change**. Not against technology, but for **alter-technologies and alter-AI**.
- **From the East, a New Europe.**
Thinking of our continent as a space of possibilities means looking towards where things are moving. In recent years, **Eastern Europe** has emerged as a **dynamic terrain of conflict**, crossed by war but also by **mass social movements that have challenged power and the status quo**. Together with those who have experienced these movements, we ask ourselves what desires and aspirations have driven these different upheavals, and whether we can **build our new Europe from the force they have generated, together with those who have carried them forward**.
- **Networks of Urban Resistance.**
The need to **move beyond the nation-state and the violence it produces** pushes us, in Europe, to think about how to **confederate rebellious cities and territories** through their networks of conflictual mutual aid, popular clinics, housing struggles, trade unionism, and demands for a European basic income — moving beyond municipalism **towards a network of urban and territorial resistance**.
